History

In 1992, two friends, Marcus Vance and Elena Cruz, launched a bold dream with two patched surplus rafts and a beat-up pickup truck. Driven by a passion for the wild canyon currents, they began guiding small, adventurous groups down the turbulent river gorges. By 1998, their rogue, word-of-mouth operation officially became Rafting Travel,

establishing a permanent basecamp at the edge of the rapids. Over thirty years, the gritty passion project evolved into the region's premier whitewater outfitter. While the gear upgraded to state-of-the-art fleets and the crew grew to dozens of elite certified guides, the core mission never shifted: to safely bridge the gap between human grit and untamed nature.
